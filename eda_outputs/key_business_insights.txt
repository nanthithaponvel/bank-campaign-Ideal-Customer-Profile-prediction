BANK MARKETING PROJECT - KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS
======================================================================

1. The overall subscription rate is 11.27%, indicating that the campaign has a relatively small positive-response segment.

2. The highest subscription-rate job category is student, suggesting that customer occupation may be useful for campaign targeting.

3. The highest-performing contact method is cellular, indicating that contact channel may influence campaign response.

4. The strongest previous-campaign group is success, showing that previous campaign history may provide useful predictive information.

5. The highest subscription-rate month is mar, suggesting that campaign timing may be relevant.

6. The number of campaign contacts should be examined carefully because repeated contacts may not always improve response.

7. Economic indicators should be evaluated together with customer and campaign characteristics rather than interpreted as isolated business rules.
